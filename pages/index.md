# Heading Charlene Fletcher
Charlene J. Fletcher is the Frances Shera Fessler Assistant Professor of History, affiliate faculty in the Race, Gender, and Sexuality Studies (RGSS) Program at Butler University, and one of three faculty co-directors of the university's Hub for Black Affairs and Community Engagement. Charlene holds master's degrees in Criminal Justice and History from John Jay College of Criminal Justice and Brooklyn College, respectively, and a Ph.D. in History from Indiana University.
